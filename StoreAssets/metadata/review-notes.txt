PhotoFind requires no account or in-app purchase. On first launch, allow the required Apple-hosted SemanticPhotosModel v1 pack to download (about 388 MB installed), then grant access to test photos and tap Start Indexing. Initial indexing may take time; keep the app open for the first test. Photos and search queries are processed on-device. Indexed local photos can be searched offline after setup. iCloud-only originals may require a network connection. Try a query that describes your test photos, or open Color Universe to browse by color. Selection supports sharing, creating albums and deletion with confirmation. English, Simplified Chinese and Light/Dark/System appearance are supported. Store screenshots use licensed demonstration photos imported into the iOS Simulator; those photos are not bundled with the app. The first App Store submission must include the required model asset pack. The developer reports successful physical-device model-download testing.
