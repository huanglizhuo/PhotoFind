Report a problem or suggest a feature

Include your iOS version, app version, what you expected, and steps to reproduce. Issues are public: do not attach private photos, device identifiers, credentials or unsanitized logs.

Getting started

Keep an internet connection for the initial model download (about 388 MB) and allow enough free space for the model and index. Grant photo access, then let the first indexing pass finish. The phone may become warm; charging can help during a large first pass. Subsequent indexing normally handles only new or changed photos.

Search and recovery

You can type while intelligent search prepares. The latest query runs when preparation finishes; clearing or closing search cancels it. Try a short description such as “sunset by the sea”. If a photo is missing, check photo permissions and indexing progress. iCloud-only photos need a connection when downloading is enabled. For download failures, restore connectivity and use Retry. You can manage the index in the app without deleting your originals.

Deleting photos

Deletion requires confirmation and affects the Photos library. Use Apple's Photos app > Recently Deleted to recover photos while recovery is still available.

帮助与反馈

报告问题或提出建议，请说明 iOS 版本、App 版本、预期结果和复现步骤。反馈公开可见，请勿附上私人照片、设备标识、密钥或未经清理的日志。

首次使用需联网下载约 388 MB 的模型，并留出模型及索引空间。授权照片后等待首次索引完成；照片较多时手机可能变热，可连接电源。以后通常只处理新增或修改过的照片。

智能搜索准备期间可以输入，准备完成后会搜索最新关键词；清空或关闭会取消。可尝试“海边的日落”等简短描述。找不到照片时，请检查权限和索引进度。iCloud 照片下载需要联网，下载失败可恢复网络后重试。管理索引不会删除照片原件。

删除操作会更改系统相册，需二次确认。如需恢复，请在系统相册“最近删除”仍可恢复时操作。
