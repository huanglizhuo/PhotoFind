Privacy policy · 隐私政策

Effective: 18 October 2026. Developer: Huang Lizhuo.

On your device

PhotoFind uses the photos you authorize to create a local search index, including image embeddings, recognized screenshot text, color information and photo identifiers. Photo location metadata may be used for local location search. Search text, recent searches, settings and indexes remain on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your photos, search queries or indexes, and do not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Network access

The first use downloads an AI model hosted by Apple. Photos stored only in iCloud may be downloaded by Apple's Photos services when you choose to access them or enable that indexing option. These requests are governed by Apple's services and privacy policy. Browsing this support website uses GitHub Pages, whose hosting service may process ordinary request information such as IP addresses.

Your choices

You can grant full or limited photo access, or revoke access in iOS Settings. Photo deletion requires confirmation and changes the system photo library, including its iCloud synchronization where enabled; recovery is managed in Photos > Recently Deleted. Sharing is initiated by you and sends the selected content to the destination you choose. Clear recent searches and manage indexes in the app. Removing the app removes its app-local data; it does not delete your photo library.

Diagnostics and contact

Apple may provide crash or diagnostic information according to your Apple settings. If you contact us through GitHub Issues, the information you post is public. Do not post personal photos or sensitive information. Contact support. Changes to this policy will be published here.

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 18 日。开发者：Huang Lizhuo。

一句找图使用你授权的照片，在本机生成搜索索引，包括图片嵌入、截图识别文字、颜色信息及照片标识。照片位置元数据可用于本地地点搜索。搜索内容、历史记录、设置和索引保留在设备上。我们没有接收照片、搜索内容或索引的服务器，也未集成广告或第三方统计服务。

首次使用需要从 Apple 下载 AI 模型。你主动打开 iCloud 照片或开启对应索引选项时，Apple 照片服务可能从 iCloud 下载原图，适用 Apple 的服务与隐私政策。本支持网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，托管服务可能处理 IP 地址等常规请求信息。

你可以只授权部分照片，并随时在 iOS 设置撤销权限。删除照片需要确认，会更改系统照片图库及其已启用的 iCloud 同步；恢复操作由系统相册的“最近删除”管理。分享由你主动发起，内容交给你选择的目标。可在 App 中清空搜索历史、管理索引。卸载 App 会移除其本地数据，不会删除照片图库。

Apple 可能按你的系统设置提供崩溃或诊断信息。通过 GitHub Issues 反馈的内容公开可见，请勿上传私人照片或敏感信息。联系支持。政策更新会在此公布。
