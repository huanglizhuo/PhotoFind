A few things to know

Can I download PhotoFind yet?

PhotoFind is preparing for launch. The first version is planned to be free. An App Store download link will appear here when it is available.

Do I always need an internet connection?

You need internet for the initial model download. After setup, indexed local photos can be searched offline. Accessing or choosing to download iCloud-only photos still requires a connection.

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

We do not operate a server that receives photos, searches, or indexes. The initial model download uses Apple services. Accessing iCloud photos or enabling their download uses Apple’s Photos services.

Why is a photo missing from search?

Check photo permissions and indexing progress. Photos without access, photos still waiting to be indexed, and undownloaded iCloud-only photos may not appear. Results may also differ from what you had in mind.

Can I recover a deleted photo?

Deleting requires confirmation and changes the system photo library. Use Recently Deleted in Apple Photos while recovery is still available.
